Make Scripture part of your day.

Read like a real book

Turn pages with your finger or open a two-page spread in landscape. Choose from the King James Version, Berean Standard Bible, and Reina-Valera 1909, on paper, linen, marble or dark pages.

Keep the verses that stay with you

Highlight in six colours, underline, heart a verse, or add a note. Bookmarks follow you from one translation to another.

Listen while you read

Have any chapter read aloud in the voice you choose, each word lighting up as it is spoken. Set a reading timer for a quiet few minutes.

Every note, in one place

Your notes appear as cards, newest first. Pin the ones you come back to, and narrow them down to a single book with one tap. Search finds any word in your notes and highlights.

Made for iPad and Apple Pencil.

Draw across a verse to select it, and a curved menu of colours and actions opens right at the tip of your Pencil.

Write in the margins

Notes can be typed or handwritten, and a finger still turns the page while the Pencil marks it.

  • Palm rejection while you write
  • Hover to preview, with haptic feedback
  • Arrow keys and trackpad turn pages

Stay with it.

Small, steady encouragement to come back tomorrow.

Build a daily reading habit

Follow a reading plan, see your streak, and watch your tree grow as you read through the Old and New Testaments.

Medals worth earning

Ninety-five medals for days read, hours spent, books finished and plans completed. Turn each one over in 3D to see when you earned it.

On your Lock Screen and wrist

Today's reading lives on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as you go. On Apple Watch, read a chapter, check it off, and see the verse of the day on your watch face.
