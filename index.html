Reading

Reads like a real book.

Pages turn under your finger, and in landscape two pages sit side by side like an open Bible. The words of Jesus are in red.

King James Version, Berean Standard Bible and Reina-Valera 1909, on paper, linen, marble or dark pages.

Study

Mark what matters.

Highlight in six colours, underline, heart a verse, or write a note — with Apple Pencil on iPad. Bookmarks follow you across translations.

Habit

Grow a little every day.

Follow a reading plan, keep a streak, and watch a tree grow as you read. Medals mark the milestones, and today's reading waits on your Lock Screen.

iPad

Room to read and write.

A wide, calm page on iPad, with handwriting, a hardware keyboard and trackpad, and everything at a glance on Home.

Apple Watch

Today's reading, on your wrist.

Read a chapter, check it off, and see the verse of the day — right from your watch face.

🗒️

Notes as cards

Pin favourites and filter by book.

👥

Read with friends

Share highlights and read a plan together — optional.

Day and night

Paper textures and dark pages that are easy on the eyes.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. It all stays in your iCloud.
