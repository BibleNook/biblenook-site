A quiet place to read Scripture.

Bible Nook turns pages like a real book, keeps your highlights and notes close, and helps you read a little every day — on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Download on the App Store

Reads like a book

Real page turns, two-page spreads in landscape, and words of Jesus in red. KJV, Berean Standard Bible and Reina-Valera 1909.

Highlight and take notes

Colours, underlines, handwritten notes with Apple Pencil, and bookmarks that follow you across translations.

Build a daily habit

Reading plans, a streak, a tree that grows as you read, and medals to earn — plus a Live Activity for today's reading.

On your wrist

Read today's chapters, check them off, and see the verse of the day on Apple Watch.

Read with friends

Optional Community: share highlights, see friends' streaks and read plans together, all through iCloud.

Private by design

No account and no tracking. Everything stays in your own iCloud.
