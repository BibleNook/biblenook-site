Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Bible Nook is built so that your reading stays yours. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't track you or show ads.

What stays on your devices and in your iCloud

Your highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading plans, reading time, streak, medals and settings are stored on your device and synced to your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. We can't see this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Community (optional)

If you turn on Community and add friends or join a group, Bible Nook shares — through iCloud sharing, directly with the people you invite — the name and animal avatar you choose, your reading streak, tree and medals, the passages you choose to share, hearts you give, and your progress in plans you read together. Your notes are never shared. People you share with can see this information; we can't. You can remove friends, leave groups, block people, or turn Community off at any time.

Apple Watch and Live Activities

Your plan progress and verse of the day are passed between your iPhone and Apple Watch, and shown on your Lock Screen, on your own devices only.

Diagnostics

Bible Nook keeps crash and performance reports on your device. They're only sent to us if you choose to share them from Settings.

Reports

If you report someone in Community, your email app opens with the report addressed to us. We use it only to review the report.

Children

Bible Nook doesn't knowingly collect information from children. Community sharing happens only between people who invite each other.

Deleting your data

Settings › Reset All Data erases everything Bible Nook has saved, on your devices and in your iCloud. Deleting the app and its iCloud data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud also removes it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@biblenook.app.
