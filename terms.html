Be kind

Don't post or share anything hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, threatening, or illegal, or anything meant to deceive or spam. Don't pretend to be someone else.

Report and block

Use Block to stop seeing someone and end any friendship with them. Use Report to tell us about content or behaviour that breaks these terms. We review reports within 24 hours and may remove content or end someone's access to Community.
