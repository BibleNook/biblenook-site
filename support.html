Common questions

My highlights aren't on my other devices.

Bible Nook syncs through iCloud. Check that you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, and that iCloud Drive is on in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Community says to sign in to iCloud.

Community connects friends through iCloud, so it needs an Apple Account signed in to iCloud on the device.

How do I turn pages with a keyboard?

Use the left and right arrow keys or Space. ⌘B opens the book list, ⌘1–4 switch between Home, Reader, Plans and Notes, and ⌘F searches.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Reset All Data removes every note, highlight, bookmark, plan and medal from your devices and iCloud. To leave Community, remove your friends and leave your groups, or turn Community off in Settings.

Where do the Bible texts come from?

See Settings › Bible Texts & Credits in the app.
